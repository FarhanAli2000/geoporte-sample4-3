Dr Mazin Alhamrany

  • PhD Geotechnical Engineering, United Kingdom
  • MSc Geotechnical Engineering
  • BSc Civil Engineering
  • Chartered Civil Engineer
  • Member of the National Information and Technology Platform for Transport and Public Space (CROW), Netherland

Mazin brings over 30 years of unparalleled experience in geotechnical and tunnelling design, having led and managed challenging projects across the UK, Europe, Middle East and Australia.

Mazin has an impressive track record, including the design and management of mega projects such as the Doha Metro, Riyadh Metro, and Etihad Rail. His extensive expertise in advanced numerical modelling and complex geotechnical problem-solving will be a tremendous asset to our team and clients.

Mazin has significant contributions to research and teaching in the field of advanced numerical methods for geotechnical problems and have written several papers on the subject.

Some of his notable projects include:

  • Crossrail Project (Farringdon Station and Pudding Mill Lane Portal)
  • Amsterdam Metro North-South Line
  • Mega Reservoirs in Doha
  • The Inner Doha Re-sewerage Implementation Strategy (IDRIS)
  • Strategic Tunnel Enhancement Programme (STEP) in Abu Dhabi

Specialisation

  • Advanced Numerical Modelling and Soil Structure Interaction Analysis
  • Trenchless Technology and Impact Assessment on Existing Structures
  • 2D and 3D Finite Element Modelling using Advanced Soil Models to Simulate Construction Staging
  • Geotechnical Risk Assessment and Management
